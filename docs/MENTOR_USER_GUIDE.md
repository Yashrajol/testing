# Vedhkrit Learner Development OS — Mentor User Guide
**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Target Audience:** Academic Mentors, Advisors & Counselors  
**Access Portal:** `https://app.vedhkrit.com`  

---

## Welcome to Vedhkrit for Mentors

Welcome to **Vedhkrit**! As an Academic Mentor, Advisor, or Counselor, Vedhkrit provides you with real-time cohort telemetry, diagnostic intelligence, and automated intervention tools to guide your mentees **"From Potential to Purpose."**

---

## 1. Login Procedure

1. Navigate to `https://app.vedhkrit.com/login`.
2. Enter your assigned **Mentor Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In to Portal"**.
4. You will automatically land on your **Mentor Dashboard** (`/dashboard/mentor`).

---

## 2. Mentor Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard serves as your daily cohort command center:
- **Total Mentees Stat Card:** Displays total students assigned to your advisement.
- **Active Risk Alerts:** Flags mentees needing urgent academic or cognitive support (`2 Urgent Alerts`).
- **Upcoming Sessions Widget:** Shows scheduled 1-on-1 and group mentoring sessions.
- **Cohort Maturity Index:** Aggregate weekly developmental growth percentage (`84.2%`).

---

## 3. Student & Cohort Management (`/dashboard/mentor/students`)

1. Click **Student Roster** in the sidebar.
2. Search mentees by name, grade, or developmental stage.
3. Click on any student profile card to inspect their complete developmental file.

---

## 4. Learning DNA & Aptitude Analysis

1. In any mentee profile, navigate to the **Learning DNA** tab.
2. Review spider radar charts showing scores across 6 cognitive dimensions:
   - Logical-Mathematical Reasoning
   - Visual-Spatial Perception
   - Linguistic Communication
   - Analytical Focus
   - Creative Problem Solving
   - Social-Emotional Wellbeing
3. Identify cognitive strengths and align career recommendations accordingly.

---

## 5. Session Scheduling (`/dashboard/mentor/sessions`)

1. Navigate to **Sessions** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ Schedule Session"**.
3. Select target student or group cohort.
4. Set session date, time, duration, and video meeting link.
5. Click **"Save & Send Invitations"**.

---

## 6. Attendance & Engagement Tracking

Review mentee attendance logs across school check-ins, mandatory classes, and advisory sessions. Flag unexcused absences directly to parents.

---

## 7. Assignments & Homework Review

1. Click **Assignments Review** in the sidebar.
2. View pending homework submissions from your mentee cohort.
3. Review attached student files, enter grades, and provide feedback notes.

---

## 8. Diagnostic Assessment Review (`/dashboard/mentor/assessments`)

1. Select **Assessments** from the menu.
2. Inspect completed diagnostic surveys submitted by mentees.
3. Analyze automated cognitive breakdowns and approve stage maturity progressions.

---

## 9. Mentor Notes & Observation Logging

1. Open a student's profile and click **"Add Mentor Note"**.
2. Document academic observations, behavioral notes, or parental interaction summaries.
3. Toggle note visibility (**Internal Only** vs. **Shared with Parent**).

---

## 10. Personalized Action Plans

1. Select **Action Plans** from the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ Create Action Plan"**.
3. Set target goals (e.g., *Mathematics Remediation Sprints*), milestone dates, and daily study targets.
4. Assign the plan to the student and track progress bars in real time.

---

## 11. Veda AI Insights & Recommendations

Use Veda AI Telemetry to receive automated suggestions for mentee interventions based on diagnostic scores, attendance changes, and homework completion rates.

---

## 12. Generating & Exporting Cohort Reports

1. Click the **"Export Data / Reports"** button on any dashboard view.
2. Select **PDF**, **Excel**, **CSV**, or **Print** in the `ExportModal`.
3. Download the generated cohort progress report for faculty review meetings.

---

## 13. Notifications & Priority Alerts

Click the bell icon to view real-time dispatches:
- High-priority student risk alerts
- Mentee assignment submissions
- Parent message requests

---

## 14. Best Practices for Academic Mentors

1. **Review Risk Alerts Daily:** Address flagged students within 24 hours.
2. **Document Transparent Notes:** Provide encouraging, constructive feedback shared with parents.
3. **Set Measurable Action Plans:** Break down large goals into 2-week actionable study sprints.

---

## 15. Troubleshooting Guide

- **Student Missing from Roster:** Contact your School Administrator to verify mentee assignment bindings.
- **Session Link Errors:** Ensure full URL (including `https://`) is entered when scheduling meeting links.
