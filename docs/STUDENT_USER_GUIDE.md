# Vedhkrit Learner Development OS — Student User Guide
**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Target Audience:** Students & Learners  
**Platform Access URL:** `https://app.vedhkrit.com`  

---

## 1. Welcome to Vedhkrit
Welcome to **Vedhkrit**, your personalized **Learner Development Operating System**. Vedhkrit is designed to support you across your complete learning journey—from daily study planning and focus timer sessions to cognitive self-assessments, career exploration, and AI-powered mentoring.

---

## 2. System & Device Requirements
Vedhkrit runs on modern desktop web browsers, tablets, and smartphones.

- **Supported Browsers:** Google Chrome (v100+), Mozilla Firefox (v100+), Apple Safari (v15+), Microsoft Edge (v100+).
- **Internet Connection:** Broadband / 4G / 5G recommended (Core features available **Offline** via PWA).
- **Display Resolution:** Optimized for mobile (375px+) to 4K desktop viewports.

---

## 3. Login Procedure

```
[ Step 1: Open https://app.vedhkrit.com/login ]
                       |
[ Step 2: Enter Email & Password Credentials ]
                       |
[ Step 3: Click "Sign In to Portal" ]
                       |
[ Step 4: Automatic Redirect to /dashboard/student ]
```

1. Navigate to `https://app.vedhkrit.com/login`.
2. Enter your student email address and password.
3. Click **"Sign In to Portal"**.
4. Upon authentication, you will be landed on your personal **Student Dashboard**.

---

## 4. Student Dashboard Overview

![Screenshot Placeholder: Student Portal Dashboard Overview](/assets/screenshots/student_dashboard_overview.png)

Your Student Dashboard is your daily learning command center:
- **Daily Streak Counter:** Tracks consecutive days of platform activity.
- **Focus Timer (Pomodoro):** 25-minute focus timer to manage study sprints.
- **Academic Progress Card:** Displays current GPA / academic percentage and attendance rate.
- **Daily Checklist:** List of homework items and planner tasks due today.

---

## 5. Profile Management (`/dashboard/student/profile`)

1. Click on your avatar in the top-right header and select **"Profile"**.
2. View your personal details, grade level, school registration ID, and assigned mentor.
3. Update bio notes, emergency contact preferences, or change password.

---

## 6. Learning DNA (`/dashboard/student/skillset`)

Your **Learning DNA** provides a visual breakdown of your cognitive strengths across 6 core baseline dimensions:
- Logical-Mathematical Reasoning
- Visual-Spatial Perception
- Linguistic Aptitude
- Interpersonal Communication
- Analytical Focus
- Creative Synthesis

---

## 7. Academics Hub (`/dashboard/student/academics`)

View your subject-by-subject performance, timetable schedule, teacher notes, and exam schedules.

---

## 8. Assignments & Homework (`/dashboard/student/assignments`)

1. Select **Assignments** from the left sidebar.
2. Filter assignments by **Pending**, **Submitted**, or **Graded**.
3. Click on any assignment card to view instructions, attach homework files, or type online responses.
4. Click **"Submit Assignment"**. (If offline, submissions auto-queue and sync when reconnected).

---

## 9. Diagnostic Self-Assessments (`/dashboard/student/assessments`)

1. Click **Assessments** in the sidebar.
2. Select any active diagnostic survey (e.g., *Cognitive Baseline Survey*).
3. Complete the multi-choice questions.
4. Upon completion, instant visual scores and mentor feedback will render.

---

## 10. Daily Planner & Study Habits (`/dashboard/student/planner`)

1. Navigate to **Planner**.
2. Click **"+ Add New Task"**.
3. Set task title, subject tag, due time, and priority level.
4. Check off tasks as you complete them to build your daily streak.

---

## 11. Live Sessions (`/dashboard/student/sessions`)

View upcoming live tutoring and mentoring sessions, join meeting links, or watch recorded archives.

---

## 12. Veda AI Assistant & Career Guidance (`/dashboard/student/career`)

1. Navigate to **Career Explorer** or click the **Veda AI Chat** icon in the header.
2. Ask questions about subject topics, exam preparation tips, or career pathways.
3. Explore match percentages for careers aligned with your Learning DNA.

---

## 13. Notifications & Alerts

Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar to view notifications for:
- New homework dispatches
- Upcoming assessment due dates
- Mentor feedback messages
- School announcements

---

## 14. Performance Analytics (`/dashboard/student/reports`)

View interactive charts showing your academic trends over time, subject strengths, and weekly study hour distributions.

---

## 15. Exporting Reports & Downloads

1. Click the **"Export Data / Reports"** button.
2. Select desired format in the **ExportModal**: **PDF**, **Excel**, **CSV**, or **Print**.
3. Click **"Generate Report"** to download your progress document.

---

## 16. PWA Installation (Desktop & Mobile App)

Vedhkrit can be installed directly as a Progressive Web App:
- **Chrome / Edge Desktop:** Click the **Install Icon** in the address bar or click **"Install App"** on the floating banner.
- **iOS Safari:** Tap the **Share Icon** -> Select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Android Chrome:** Tap the **Three Dots Menu** -> Select **"Install App"**.

---

## 17. Working in Offline Mode

When your device loses internet connectivity:
- A yellow status banner appears: *"Working Offline Mode — Local cache enabled for assignments, notes & study resources."*
- You can continue creating study notes, completing assignment drafts, and viewing cached resources.
- Once connectivity is restored, all saved drafts automatically sync to the server.

---

## 18. Troubleshooting Guide

- **Login Fails:** Verify your email spelling and check Caps Lock on password.
- **Page Not Updating:** Click the **Update Now** banner or perform a hard refresh (`Ctrl + Shift + R`).
- **Offline Sync Issues:** Ensure browser LocalStorage permission is allowed.

---

## 19. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: How do I reset my password?**  
  *A: Click "Forgot Password?" on the login screen or request a reset link from your school administrator.*
- **Q: Can my parent see my daily planner tasks?**  
  *A: Parents can see overall progress, attendance, and assessment reports, but your daily study notes remain private.*

---

## 20. Best Practices for Students

1. **Log in daily:** Maintain your daily streak to earn developmental milestone badges.
2. **Use the Focus Timer:** Work in 25-minute Pomodoro study sprints to maximize retention.
3. **Keep your Planner updated:** Add assignment due dates as soon as teachers assign them.
