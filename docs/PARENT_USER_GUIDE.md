# Vedhkrit Learner Development OS — Parent User Guide
**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Target Audience:** Parents & Guardians  
**Access Portal:** `https://app.vedhkrit.com`  

---

## Welcome to Vedhkrit for Parents

Welcome to **Vedhkrit**! As a parent or guardian, Vedhkrit provides you with clear, real-time insights into your child's educational journey—not just their exam marks, but their attendance, cognitive growth, daily habits, and mentor guidance.

---

## 1. Logging In to Your Account

1. Open your web browser on your phone, tablet, or computer and go to `https://app.vedhkrit.com/login`.
2. Type in your registered **Parent Email** and **Password**.
3. Click the blue **"Sign In to Portal"** button.
4. You will automatically land on your **Parent Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Child Overview Dashboard (`/dashboard/parent`)

Your dashboard gives you a quick snapshot of your child's overall wellbeing and school performance:
- **Child Card:** Displays your child's name, grade level, and school campus.
- **Academic Summary:** Overall subject average percentage.
- **Attendance Rate:** Percentage of school days attended.
- **Recent Mentor Feedback:** Quick notes from your child's assigned advisor.

*(If you have multiple children enrolled in partner schools, you can switch between them using the child drop-down menu at the top of the dashboard).*

---

## 3. Attendance Tracking (`/dashboard/parent/attendance`)

1. Click **Attendance** on the left menu.
2. View monthly calendar views showing **Present**, **Absent**, and **Excused** days.
3. Review exact timestamps for morning arrival and school check-in.

---

## 4. Academic Progress (`/dashboard/parent/progress`)

1. Click **Progress & Academics** on the left menu.
2. View interactive charts showing score progression over term exams.
3. Review homework submission rates and subject-by-subject breakdowns (Mathematics, Science, English, Social Studies).

---

## 5. Learning DNA Insights

Vedhkrit measures 6 baseline cognitive dimensions to help you understand how your child learns best:
- **Logical-Mathematical Reasoning**
- **Visual-Spatial Understanding**
- **Language & Communication**
- **Focus & Analytical Attention**
- **Creative Problem Solving**
- **Social-Emotional Balance**

Understanding your child's Learning DNA allows you to encourage their natural strengths without creating unnecessary academic pressure.

---

## 6. Growth Reports & Mentor Notes (`/dashboard/parent/mentor`)

1. Select **Mentor & Feedback** from the sidebar menu.
2. Read personalized observations written by your child's assigned mentor.
3. Review personalized action plans created to support areas needing improvement.

---

## 7. Direct Communication Features (`/dashboard/parent/messages`)

1. Click **Messages** on the left menu.
2. Send direct text messages to your child's mentor or class coordinator.
3. Receive meeting invitations for parent-teacher conferences.

---

## 8. Downloading & Exporting Progress Reports

1. Click the **"Export Data / Reports"** button located on any report page.
2. A window will pop up allowing you to choose your format:
   - **PDF:** Best for printing or keeping official records.
   - **Excel / CSV:** Best for viewing raw numbers.
   - **Print:** Prints directly to your home printer.
3. Click **"Generate Report"** to download the file instantly.

---

## 9. Installing Vedhkrit on Your Smartphone or Tablet (PWA)

You can install Vedhkrit like a regular mobile app without going to an App Store:
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Open `https://app.vedhkrit.com` -> Tap the **Share Icon** (box with arrow) -> Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Android Phone (Chrome):** Open `https://app.vedhkrit.com` -> Tap the **Three Dots Menu** -> Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.

---

## 10. Using Vedhkrit Without Internet (Offline Access)

If you lose internet connection while using the app:
- A banner will appear at the top: *"Working Offline Mode"*.
- You can still view previously loaded report cards, attendance history, and mentor notes.
- The app updates automatically as soon as you reconnect to Wi-Fi or mobile data.

---

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: How do I change my password?**  
  *A: Click on your name in the top right corner, select "Profile Settings", and click "Change Password".*
- **Q: Can I grant access to another family member?**  
  *A: Yes. Contact your school administrator to add a secondary parent or guardian email to your account.*

---

## 12. Troubleshooting Guide

- **Forgot Password:** Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page to receive a password reset link by email.
- **Page Is Not Loading:** Ensure you have an active Wi-Fi or mobile data connection, or perform a page refresh.
