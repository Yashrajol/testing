# Vedhkrit Learner Development OS — School Administrator Guide
**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Target Audience:** School Principals, Directors & Administrative Officers  
**Access Portal:** `https://app.vedhkrit.com`  

---

## Welcome to Vedhkrit for School Administrators

Welcome to **Vedhkrit**! As a School Principal, Director, or Administrative Officer, Vedhkrit provides you with complete institutional telemetry, faculty management tools, developmental analytics, and administrative controls to manage your campus efficiently.

---

## 1. Institution Setup (`/dashboard/admin/profile`)

1. Log in using your school admin credentials (`admin@your-school.edu.in`).
2. Navigate to **School Profile**.
3. Upload official institution logo, campus address, principal contact details, and accreditation numbers.
4. Set official term dates and grading scale metrics.

---

## 2. Student Management (`/dashboard/admin/students`)

1. Click **Student Directory** in the sidebar.
2. Search, filter, or add new student enrollments.
3. Click **"Bulk Import CSV"** to upload full grade enrollments.
4. Assign students to specific batches, classes, and academic mentors.

---

## 3. Parent Management

1. View parent contact profiles linked to enrolled students.
2. Re-send portal access invitations or update primary guardian contact information.

---

## 4. Mentor & Faculty Management (`/dashboard/admin/mentors` & `/dashboard/admin/teachers`)

1. Click **Faculty & Mentors** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ Add Faculty Member"**.
3. Enter name, professional email, role (`teacher` or `mentor`), and assigned subjects.
4. Bind mentors to specific student advisement cohorts.

---

## 5. Courses & Batch Management

1. Navigate to **Courses & Curriculum**.
2. Define academic departments (Science, Mathematics, Humanities, Technology).
3. Create student batches (e.g., *Grade 10 - Section A (2026 Batch)*).

---

## 6. School Attendance Tracking

1. View real-time daily campus attendance percentage graphs (`96.2%`).
2. Inspect unexcused absence reports and send automated SMS/Email alerts to parents.

---

## 7. Institutional Diagnostic Assessments

1. Monitor school-wide diagnostic survey completion rates.
2. Analyze baseline cognitive radar charts across departments.

---

## 8. School Announcements & Basic CMS (`/dashboard/admin/cms`)

1. Click **Announcements & CMS** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ New Announcement"**.
3. Enter title, target audience (**All**, **Students Only**, **Parents Only**, **Mentors Only**), and notice text.
4. Click **"Publish Announcement"**.

---

## 9. Institution Analytics (`/dashboard/admin/analytics`)

View high-level institutional intelligence charts:
- Overall Developmental Growth Index (`82.4 / 100`)
- ILDF Developmental Stage Spread (Stage 1 to Stage 4)
- Departmental Academic Averages

---

## 10. Reports & Data Exports

1. Click the **"Export Data / Reports"** button on any administrative view.
2. Select **PDF**, **Excel**, **CSV**, or **Print** in the `ExportModal`.
3. Export complete school roster, attendance logs, or development index summaries.

---

## 11. Membership & Subscription (`/dashboard/admin/membership`)

View active platform license tier, student slot capacity (`1,250 / 2,500 slots used`), and renewal dates.

---

## 12. Security & RBAC Enforcement

- **Role Protection:** School Admin privileges restrict access to institution-level data only.
- **Audit Logging:** System records all admin modifications (user additions, role changes, exports).
- **Session Expiration:** Inactive sessions auto-terminate after 15 minutes.

---

## 13. Backup Procedures

- School data is automatically backed up daily to encrypted cloud storage with Point-In-Time Recovery (RPO < 15 min).

---

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: How do I increase our student slot capacity?**  
  *A: Contact your Vedhkrit Account Representative or Super Admin to upgrade your subscription tier.*
- **Q: Can teachers see mentor notes?**  
  *A: Teachers can view public academic notes, but confidential advisement notes are restricted to assigned mentors.*
